Our story

Built around the dogs
we know by name.

Founded in 2017, VolPets began with a simple belief: dogs thrive when the people caring for them truly know them. That belief shapes our membership-led approach to dog walking and training in London.

Small groups, familiar handlers and consistent routines help us understand each dog's personality, confidence and behaviour over time.

Our people

A trainer of trainers.
One trusted team.

Founder and lead trainer Fabian Volpe trained with Cesar Millan and Victoria Stilwell, earning international credentials. As a trainer of trainers, he shares that expertise through in-house mentoring in canine body language, handling and group management. Every team member is certified in Canine First Aid & CPR.
