Our services

Everything your dog needs,
considered in every detail.

From weekday dog walking to one-to-one training and members-only home boarding, choose support that fits your dog and your routine. Contact us to discuss availability and what your membership includes.

Dog walking

Weekday 90-minute dog walks in Kensington Gardens, with up to four dogs per handler. Carefully matched groups combine exercise, enrichment and guided socialisation. Off-lead time is tailored to each dog.

Dog training

One-to-one dog training for recall, lead walking and everyday manners, with practical coaching for owners. Dogs and owners receive a VolPets certificate on completing their training programme

Dog behaviour support

Individual support for reactivity and challenging behaviour. We assess your dog's needs, agree practical goals and guide you through a tailored training plan.

Home-to-home boarding

Members-only home boarding with a team your dog already knows. A familiar routine and personal care in our own home, with availability agreed in advance.

Vet concierge

Support with your dog's vet appointments, including agreed transport, check-ins and updates. Ask us what is included in your membership and how additional visits are arranged.

Health & preventative care

Help keeping track of vaccination dates, parasite treatment schedules and routine vet appointments. We coordinate agreed care with you, following your vet's advice.

Canine Reiki

Optional canine Reiki sessions for owners who wish to include them in their dog's care. A complementary service, separate from veterinary treatment and behaviour training.

Private pet transport

Private dog transport for vet visits, grooming and agreed journeys in London and beyond. Climate-controlled vehicles and a familiar team help keep your dog's routine consistent.
