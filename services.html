Our services

Everything your dog needs,
considered in every detail.

From daily adventures to specialist care, our range of services is designed to support your dog's physical health, behaviour and overall wellbeing.

Dog walking

Daily 90-minute off-leash walks in the park with carefully matched groups of up to 4 dogs. Training, enrichment and healthy socialisation built into every adventure.

Dog training

Private 1-to-1 sessions tailored to your dog's needs. From everyday manners to recall, focus and more advanced training. Evidence-based, kind and effective.

Behavioural rehabilitation

Specialist support for reactive, aggressive and challenging dogs. Our trainers create personalised plans to help dogs feel calmer, safer and more confident.

Home-to-home boarding

Exclusive boarding for VolPets dogs in the comfort of our own home. A calm, loving environment where your dog is treated like part of the family.

Vet concierge

We take care of up to two vet visits per month on your behalf, including transport, check-ins and updates, so your dog's health is always in the best hands.

Health & preventative care

From vaccination reminders to parasite treatments and annual health checks, we manage and coordinate your dog's preventative healthcare so nothing is missed.

Canine Reiki

Professional Reiki sessions to support emotional balance, reduce stress and promote overall wellbeing. Ideal for sensitive, anxious or recovering dogs.

Private pet transport

Safe, comfortable and climate-controlled transport for vet visits, grooming, special appointments or travel across London and beyond.
